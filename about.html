About Me

I am a final-year Computer Science student at the Tshwane University of Technology, Polokwane Campus. I am passionate about technology and problem-solving, and I believe in making the world a better place through innovation and the sharing of ideas.

During my time at university, I have served as a Student Development and Support Mental Health Champion and Mentor, helping students overcome personal and academic challenges. I have also served as the Deputy Secretary for the ICT House Committee, working with class representatives to address student concerns within the faculty.

In 2024, I attended the 3rd Annual Limpopo Varsity Hackathon and won 1st place with my team. The following year, 2025, I attended the 4th Annual Limpopo Varsity Hackathon and won 1st place again with a different team, achieving back-to-back victories over two consecutive years.

In 2025, I was recognized as the Faculty of ICT's Top Student for obtaining an average of 91%. As the Top Achiever in the Department of Computer Science, I had the privilege of serving as a guest speaker and motivating first-year students on the merits of discipline.

I encourage all students to believe in themselves and pursue what makes them happy. I also encourage students to attend hackathons and extracurricular activities, which provide valuable experiences beyond the lecture room.

As a woman in technology, I want to inspire more women to pursue studies in the tech field to achieve gender parity and create a more balanced industry. Women are equally capable of succeeding in technology, and I am proud to contribute to that growing representation.
